About me

I am a psychologist and neuropsychologist with clinical and academic practice, working with individuals across the lifespan.

My work is grounded in scientific evidence and centered on individual experience, promoting ethical and effective mental health care.

I also work as a researcher and university lecturer, integrating clinical practice, research and education.

Services

Neuropsychological Assessment

What it is

A structured evaluation process designed to understand the relationship between brain and behavior through interviews, observation and specific instruments.

What it is for

  • Describe cognitive, affective and behavioral functioning.
  • Identify therapeutic needs and recommend interventions.
  • Support differential diagnosis and multidisciplinary work.

Psychotherapy

A safe space for care, change and self-knowledge.

What it is

A collaborative and structured process focused on emotional health care, using evidence-based interventions.

How it works

In a supportive listening space, we work together to understand your history, identify what has been undermining your well-being and build practical strategies for change.

Who it is for

Recommended for people dealing with anxiety, stress, depression and other emotional challenges in everyday life.

Clinical Supervision in Neuropsychological Assessment

Who it is for

Psychologists and students who want to improve their practice with technical safety and scientific rigor.

What we cover

Hypothesis formulation, instrument selection, data interpretation and report writing.

Research Consulting

Methodological support for scientific research in psychology, neuroscience and cognition.

  • Planning: design, protocols and ethics review.
  • Execution: data collection and quality control.
  • Analysis: statistics and quantitative methods.
  • Communication: scientific writing and publications.
